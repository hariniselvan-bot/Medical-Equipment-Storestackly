Our Story

From a two-person workshop to a global medical partner

STACKLY began in 2001 as a calibration workshop for hospital monitors. Our founders — a biomedical engineer and an ICU nurse — believed equipment should be designed around the people who use it at 3 a.m., not around a datasheet.

Today that philosophy ships in more than 10,000 devices across 40+ countries: monitors, ventilators, imaging systems and complete hospital programs, all backed by the same promise — it must work, every single time.
